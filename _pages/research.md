## Hydrologic Processes and Machine Learning

I study how hydrologic process understanding can guide runoff prediction, residual diagnostics, and interpretable machine learning. This work uses hydrologic signatures and catchment classification to examine model behavior beyond aggregate performance metrics and to identify when, where, and why predictions remain reliable.

## Causal Environmental Data Science

I use causal inference and spatiotemporal analysis to investigate environmental systems and heterogeneous effects. The emphasis is on the support for attribution, transparent treatment of uncertainty, and scientific workflows that are reproducible and auditable.

## Water–Energy–Compute Nexus

I examine the environmental implications of data centers and AI infrastructure, including direct water use, electricity-related water footprints, and supply-network attribution. This work connects infrastructure demands to hydrologic receptors and climate risk without treating accounting estimates as evidence of local impact.
