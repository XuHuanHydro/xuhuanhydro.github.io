I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Michigan School for Environment and Sustainability.

I study how hydrologic process knowledge, causal inference, and machine learning can improve environmental prediction, scientific understanding, and decision-making. My current work spans hydrologic model diagnostics, interpretable AI, catchment classification, and the water–energy–compute nexus.

## Research areas

### Hydrologic Processes and Machine Learning

Process-informed prediction, residual diagnostics, interpretable machine learning, and hydrologic signatures for understanding catchment behavior.

### Causal Environmental Data Science

Causal inference, spatiotemporal evidence, heterogeneous effects, uncertainty, and reproducible environmental analysis.

### Water–Energy–Compute Nexus

Direct and indirect water demands of energy and computing infrastructure, their supply networks, and their hydrologic context.
